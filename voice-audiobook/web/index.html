    Good to know

    • Use Chrome, or Safari 26 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon. They let the model use the graphics chip; other browsers can be many times slower.
    • Try “Hear a sample” first. A long book takes hours: plug in the charger, keep this tab open, and the screen stays awake while it works.
    • Progress is saved as you go. If the tab closes, open this page again with the same text and voice and press “Make the audiobook” to carry on.
    • Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) have no text to read. Copy the text in by hand or use a PDF with real text.
    • Only clone a voice you have the right to use: your own, or with the speaker’s permission.
